What we don't measure

  • Precision or false-positive rate on real Bluesky data: there is no ground truth there, so we only compare flag counts with chance level.
  • Performance against operators who deliberately vary their writing style.
  • Whether staged arguments between two different people (type B) can be told apart from intense genuine ones (type D) using interaction evidence alone; the ablation shows what each key contributes.
  • Non-English content.
